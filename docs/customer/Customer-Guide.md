## 2. Getting started (3 small steps)

1. **Open your app link** in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
2. **Follow the guided setup.** It asks about your currency, pay schedule, bank balance, savings, debts, goals and bills. Every question is optional, and "Use example values" is there if you're unsure. You can run it again later from **Getting Started**.
3. **Bookmark or install the app** (see section 3) so it's one tap away.

Then, each day or week:

- Tap **+** to add an expense. It takes a few seconds.
- Check **Safe-to-Spend** before you spend.
- Mark bills **Paid** when you pay them. Nothing is marked paid automatically.
- Update your **bank balance** every week or two to keep things accurate.

## 5. Where your data lives (privacy)

- Your financial information is stored **only in your browser on your device**. It is never uploaded to PixoraaGoods or anyone else.
- There is **no bank connection, no account, no ads and no tracking** inside the app.
- The hosting provider sees normal technical web requests when the app's files are downloaded. Your budget data is never part of them.

**Important limits of browser storage:**

- Data **doesn't sync** between devices or browsers. Your phone and laptop keep separate data.
- **Clearing browsing data / site data**, using **private or incognito** windows, or **uninstalling** the app can delete your data.
- On iPhone/iPad, Safari may remove data for websites you haven't used for a few weeks. Installing the app to your Home Screen and keeping backups is the safest choice.

## 6. Backups (please do this!)

**Make a backup:** Settings → **Export full backup (.json)**. Save the file somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to yourself, USB). A monthly backup is a good habit.

**Restore a backup** (or move to a new device): open the app on the new device → Settings → **Import a backup…** → choose your file → check the summary → **Replace my data**. You can press **Undo** straight after.

**Spreadsheet copy:** Settings → **Export transactions (.csv)** opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

## 7. How Safe-to-Spend works

Safe-to-Spend = **money available now** − **bills due before your next payday (or month end)** − **savings you planned but haven't moved yet** − **other money you've set aside**.

- "Money available now" is the bank balance you typed in, plus income received, minus spending, savings transfers and debt payments you've logged since then.
- **Expected paychecks are never counted** until you mark them received.
- Moving money to savings is a **transfer, not spending**.
- If information is missing (like your bank balance), the app tells you what to add instead of guessing.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| My data is gone | Did you use a different browser, a private window, or clear site data? Open the app where you used it before, or import your latest backup. |
| Numbers look wrong | Update your bank balance (Safe-to-Spend → Update balance). Check for duplicate expenses or bills that aren't marked paid. |
| Expected paycheck still shows after I got paid | Record it: Paycheck Planner → **Mark received** (or Income → Record income and link it to your regular income). |
| The app won't open offline | Open it once online first, in the same browser or installed app. |
| I don't see "Install" | Use Chrome/Edge on Android or a computer, or Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone. Firefox desktop can't install apps. |
| The screen looks old after an update | Tap **Update now** on the green banner, or close and reopen the app. |
| Something else | Message us on Etsy (below). Please include your device, browser and a screenshot. **Never send your backup file or bank details.** |
