## Description

Know what's safe to spend, without the overwhelm.

The PixoraaGoods ADHD Budget Planner is a calm budgeting app that runs in your web browser on your phone, tablet or computer. It shows you one clear number (what's safe to spend until payday), what's due soon, and one small next step. No spreadsheets, no bank connection, no subscription.

✨ WHY IT FEELS EASIER
✔️ "Your Next Small Step": one clear action, not a to-do list
✔️ Add an expense in seconds with one big + button
✔️ Safe-to-Spend: bills and savings are set aside first, and expected paychecks are never counted early
✔️ Gentle, guilt-free messages and visual progress bars
✔️ Optional 10-second daily check-in (missed days are fine)
✔️ Guided setup where every question is optional
✔️ Light and dark mode

📊 WHAT'S INSIDE (13 sections)
• Today dashboard: safe-to-spend, available money, upcoming bills, next payday, budget, savings, debt and goal progress
• Getting Started: setup checklist and guided setup
• Monthly Budget: category limits, progress bars, planned vs actual, copy last month, custom categories
• Paycheck Planner: plan each paycheck for bills, savings and spending, plus expected vs received
• Safe-to-Spend calculator with a step-by-step breakdown
• Expense tracker with search and filters
• Income tracker with regular and one-off income
• Bills & Calendar: due dates, paydays, mark as paid, past-due highlights
• Savings Goals: separate funds and history
• Debt Tracker: balances, payments, payoff estimates, Snowball vs Avalanche explained
• Financial Goals with milestones
• Reports: monthly charts, spending by category, annual overview, CSV export
• Settings: currency (USD, GBP, CAD, AUD, EUR and more), date format, themes, backups

📦 WHAT YOU'LL RECEIVE
• A PDF Quick-Start Guide containing your private link to the app
• Instructions to install it on your Home Screen or computer, use it offline, and back up your data
• A personal-use licence

💻 HOW IT WORKS
1. After purchase, go to Etsy › You › Purchases and reviews › Download files (use a web browser; the Etsy app doesn't download files).
2. Open the PDF and tap your app link.
3. Follow the short guided setup, or skip it and explore.
4. Optional: install it to your Home Screen. After the first visit it also works offline.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
• You type in your own numbers. It never connects to your bank.
• Your data is saved only in your browser on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
• Export a backup any time, and restore it on another device.

📌 PLEASE NOTE
• This is a digital product (web app access + PDF). Nothing physical is shipped.
• Data does not sync between devices or browsers. Use Export/Import backup to move it.
• Clearing browser data, private/incognito windows or uninstalling can remove your data, so please back up regularly.
• Reminders are shown inside the app. It doesn't send phone or email notifications.
• Changing currency changes the symbol only. It doesn't convert money.
• Debt payoff dates are estimates.
• Works in up-to-date Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox. Installing as an app isn't supported in desktop Firefox.
• This is a budgeting and organisation tool. It is not financial, tax, legal or medical advice, and it does not diagnose or treat ADHD.
• For personal use only. Please don't share or resell the link or files.
